7 Small Kitchen Organization Ideas That Make a Tiny Kitchen Feel Bigger

November 16, 2026

A small kitchen rarely lacks space. It lacks usable space: the back of a cabinet you can't see into, the inside of a door, the air above the counter.

The ideas below all work the same way. They move things up, onto a door, or into a box you can pull out, so the space you already have starts working.

Start with the counter

Clear counters make a small kitchen look twice as big. Anything you use less than once a day can live on a hook, a strip or a shelf instead.

Then the cabinets

Most cabinets lose half their space to a single layer of stuff at the bottom. Risers, tiered shelves and pull-out baskets fix that without any tools.
