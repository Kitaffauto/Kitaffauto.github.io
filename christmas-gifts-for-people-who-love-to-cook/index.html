The best gifts for people who love to cook are not novelty gadgets. They are the solid, everyday tools a cook uses several times a week and rarely buys for themselves.

For the cook who has everything

Upgrades beat extras. A sharp chef's knife, a cast iron skillet or a fast instant-read thermometer replaces a tool they already use with one that simply works better.

For new cooks

Start with the basics that prevent the most common mistakes: a thermometer for meat, a good knife and a set of spatulas that won't scratch pans.

Stocking stuffer ideas

A garlic rocker, a spatula set or a thermometer all fit in a stocking and get used long after the holidays.
