Fridge Organization: A Simple Zone System That Cuts Food Waste

November 17, 2026

This post contains affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Most fridge waste comes from food we simply forget. A zone system makes it obvious where things live, and what needs eating first.

Give every shelf one job

Leftovers and ready-to-eat food go up top. Dairy and eggs sit in the middle. Raw meat goes on the bottom shelf so nothing drips onto other food.

Drawers and door

Fruit and vegetables go in the drawers. The door is the warmest spot, so keep it for condiments and drinks.

Add an eat-first bin

One clear bin at eye level for anything that needs using soon. Check it before you cook.
