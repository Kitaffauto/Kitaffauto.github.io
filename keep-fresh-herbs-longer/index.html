How to Keep Fresh Herbs Green Longer: Treat Them Like Flowers

November 16, 2026

This post contains affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Soft herbs like parsley, cilantro and mint wilt fast in the bag they came in. They last much longer if you treat them like a bunch of flowers.

The jar method

  1. Snip a little off the stems.
  2. Stand the bunch in a jar with a couple of centimeters of water.
  3. Cover the leaves loosely with a bag and put the jar in the fridge.
  4. Change the water every few days.

Basil is different

Basil darkens in the cold. Keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sun.

Hardy herbs

Rosemary, thyme and sage prefer a slightly damp paper towel in a container in the fridge.
