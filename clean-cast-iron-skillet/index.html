How to Clean a Cast Iron Skillet Without Ruining It

November 17, 2026

This post contains affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Cast iron has a reputation for being fussy. The routine is actually short, and it gets easier every time you cook in the pan.

The 4-step routine

  1. Rinse the pan with hot water while it is still warm.
  2. Scrub off food with a brush, scraper or chain mail. A small drop of dish soap is fine.
  3. Dry it completely, then put it on low heat for a minute or two.
  4. Wipe on a very thin layer of oil while it's warm.

What to avoid

Don't soak it or leave it wet in the sink. Water is what causes rust, not soap.
